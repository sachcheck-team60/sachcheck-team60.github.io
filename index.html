How SachCheck works

1 · TYPEYou type or paste a message, in English, Hinglish or some Hindi words.
2 · MATCHSachCheck pulls out the keywords and looks for a myth where every key idea matches (like "lemon" + "corona").
3 · ANSWERIt shows TRUE, FALSE or UNVERIFIED, a short reason, 2 reliable sources and "Always ask a doctor".

Smart matching

  • Word families: each idea has many words. "Corona", "covid-19", "कोरोना" all mean COVID. "Haldi" = turmeric, "nimbu" = lemon, "tika/jab" = vaccine.
  • Spelling slips are OK: "vaccinatoin" or "dengu" still match (one or two letters wrong in longer words).
  • All ideas must match: "lemon cures cancer" does not match "lemon cures COVID", so we never answer the wrong question.
  • Best match wins: if two myths match, extra helper words decide (e.g. "lemon water" picks the lemon myth, not the hot-water myth).
  • Stop words: words like "sanitizer" stop the "drinking alcohol" myth from being picked by mistake.

When nothing matches

SachCheck never guesses. It says UNVERIFIED – not checked yet, asks you not to forward the message, shows close checked myths, and tells you where to check: PIB Fact Check or a doctor.

How we check our facts

Every verdict is checked with at least 2 reliable sources (WHO, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, PIB Fact Check, UNICEF and others). If sources don't agree, or proof is missing, we mark it UNVERIFIED.

SachCheck does not give medical advice. Always ask a doctor.
